# HoldOff Privacy Policy — Draft for Publication

**Last updated:** August 25, 2026

## Overview

HoldOff is an Android-first SMS messenger designed to help people pause before sending emotionally intense messages. This policy describes how the app handles information when it is installed as an Android default SMS app. A public copy must be hosted at a stable HTTPS URL and linked from the Google Play listing before release.

## Information stored on the device

HoldOff stores its SMS conversation records, message bodies, delivery states, held messages, Spiral Lock preferences, trusted-contact support configuration, drafts, people, patterns, and consent choices on the user’s device. This data is used to display the messenger, preserve a message that a user chooses to hold, and provide the features the user has configured.

HoldOff does not sell SMS content or use it for advertising. It does not upload a user’s inbox for background AI analysis.

## SMS permissions and default-messenger role

When a person chooses HoldOff as their Android default SMS app, HoldOff may request the SMS and notification permissions needed to send, receive, display, and notify about SMS messages. The Android system role chooser remains the final authority for default-SMS activation. The app’s native SMS functions are unavailable until the person grants that role and the related permissions.

## Optional contact-name matching

HoldOff shows phone numbers unless a person explicitly chooses **Show contact names** in Messages. That action requests read-only access to device contacts and matches message phone numbers with local contact names on the device. HoldOff does not create, edit, upload, or share contacts. A person can choose **Show phone numbers instead** at any time to remove locally stored conversation labels from HoldOff.

## Sadie assistance

Sadie is optional. Message content is sent to Sadie only when a person deliberately requests draft analysis, interpretation, companion chat, or conversation analysis and has enabled the related consent. Before a conversation analysis, HoldOff states whether all local messages in that thread fit within the analysis limit or how many newest messages will be included. HoldOff does not automatically submit incoming messages, held messages, or an inbox to Sadie.

Opening the Sadie companion from an active message thread does not, by itself, send that thread’s messages to Sadie. The person can chat about their moment without sharing the thread text, or separately choose the disclosed **Analyze conversation** action.

Conversation analysis is attachment-informed and non-diagnostic. It may reflect observable communication patterns as possibilities, such as a wish for reassurance, space, clarity, repair, or boundaries; it does not assign attachment styles, clinical labels, or hidden motives to either person.

## Spiral Lock and trusted-contact support

Spiral Lock is off by default. When enabled, it can hold an outgoing message before transmission when a user-selected local writing cue appears at the send decision. A separately enabled on-device emotional-cue check may show observable cues in the current draft, such as repeated punctuation or urgency wording; it does not diagnose a person, scan the inbox, or transmit content. A held message remains visible for review, release, editing, or deletion.

Trusted-contact support is separately off by default. It requires the person to choose a recipient, provide an SMS number, set an editable support message, acknowledge the feature, and enable it. When these settings are active and Spiral Lock activates, HoldOff may send the separate configured support SMS after a visible cancel window. The held message body, conversation history, Sadie chat, and interpretations are not included unless the person intentionally writes them into their own support template.

## Controls, export, and deletion

A person can disable Spiral Lock, disable trusted-contact support, change the support recipient or message, revoke AI consent, export local data, or delete local HoldOff data through Settings. Deleting local data removes HoldOff-managed records and settings from that device; it does not retract SMS messages that were already transmitted through the carrier network.

## Safety boundary

HoldOff is not therapy, emergency response, or a substitute for professional care. In the United States, people in crisis can call or text **988**. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.
